email/password and Google admission, one atomic email-bound invite redemption, one durable singleton

## In scope

- Provider-free Better Auth composition boundary and deterministic email/password and Google fixture
  identities. Email/password requires verified email; Google requires its verified email to equal the
  invite email.
- Unique, single-use, non-plaintext invite verifier bound to one normalized intended email. Code
  consume, redemption audit, and durable admission bind in one transaction. Returning admitted
  identities bypass the invite challenge.
- Additive durable singleton-session and queue repository behavior over the CP-01 contracts. First
  valid idle Generate atomically locks both receipt-bound fixture GPU selections and activates one
  entry; concurrent losers inherit the winner's pair and join as inert waiters.
- All admitted users have equal queue rights. Any admitted actor may append, optimistic-reorder, or
  remove `WAITING` entries. `ACTIVE` entries reject move/delete. Every mutation records actor plus
  old/new order and queue version.
- Preserve accepted shell geometry. Idle Create Project shows two app-native fixture GPU selectors;
  active/queued state shows only `Add to queue` plus the exact locked pair/rates. Queue views expose
  live version/position and conflict-safe feedback.
- Reuse ImageForge's current selector presentation and receipt/final-recheck principles only. Do not
  import its Tauri/device-local queue or any resource ownership.
