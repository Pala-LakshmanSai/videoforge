Label the value “Up to X% of the finished video.” Explain that whole scenes, avatar time and clip availability can reduce actual coverage. Avoid provider or GPU controls in this product flow.

A 25% choice on a 60-second video permits up to 15 seconds of generated scene footage. At 20%, that same film permits 6.6 seconds and can include a complete 5-second scene if one is eligible. The percentage is a ceiling, not a promise of exact footage duration. There is no automatic minimum-one-scene exception.
