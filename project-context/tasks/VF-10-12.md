quality, throughput, and the 30-minute variable-cost target at or below $1 and hard ceiling at $2.

## Scope

- Run the exact Mage 40-prompt/300-image suite, SoulX 12–20-clip Avatar Profile suite, five-style suite,

  idle timeout, and recovery without changing model or visual contracts. Keep `REQUEST_COUNT=1`,
  Mage 1280x720 output, and complete-batch attempt cardinality locked unless a new decision and
  requalification explicitly supersede them.
- Treat RTX 5090 as a separate optional per-lane qualification with its own exact proposal/cap; never
  add it as an unqualified automatic fallback.
