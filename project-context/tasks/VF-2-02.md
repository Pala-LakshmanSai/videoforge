# VF-2-02 — durable local transcription and span audio

Status: standing provider-free authority after VF-2-01

## Goal and dependencies

Persist real local transcription timing and materialize checksum-bound selected avatar span audio
through the committed Phase 2 contract. VF-2-01 must be green.

Read profile: `phase2_transcription`.

## Scope and ownership

- Own image-media transcription job wiring, timing persistence adapter, span-audio materializer,
  and isolated tests.
- Reuse pinned local whisper.cpp behavior; ordinary tests use fixtures and never download a model.
- Bind voiceover bytes, model/config hash, words, sentence/phrase derivation, attempts, artifacts,
  cancellation and invalidation.
- Materialize only selected padded avatar spans, never the whole voiceover for avatar workers.
- Integration owner alone edits schemas, migrations, shared repository interfaces, metadata,
  context, or commits.
- Out of scope: paid ASR, provider calls, timeline composition changes, cloud, and spend.

## Acceptance

- Timestamps are monotonic/in bounds; retries are idempotent; cancellation/invalidations leave no
  accepted orphan result.
- Span audio has deterministic boundaries/checksums and exact revision/timeline lineage.
- Focused worker/control-plane tests and full uncached verify, local-slice regression, secret scan,
  audit, and diff check pass.

Evidence: `evidence/acceptance/VF-2-02/durable-local-transcription`.
