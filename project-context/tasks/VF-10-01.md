# VF-10-01 — V2-01 tenant-private identity and data

Status: blocked until the independent V2-00 audit is green; provider-free

## Goal

Replace active global equal-rights ownership with one admitted account mapped to one default
workspace. User-created projects, presets, assets, jobs, outputs, costs, and audits are private;
explicit built-in presets are the only globally readable records.

## Scope

- Add additive ownership migrations and trusted-principal repository boundaries.
- Backfill historical rows into an explicit legacy/system scope; never rewrite old migrations.
- Apply database/query authorization to every metadata path, including existence and search.
- Preserve invite-only identity and the approved UI.
- Prove fresh, upgrade, restore, two-account, forged-ID, stale-session, and Chrome isolation cases.

No storage-provider composition, queue redesign, Serverless transport, credentials, remote mutation,
or spend belongs here.
