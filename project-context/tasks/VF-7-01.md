## In scope

- Extend the Image Style repository contracts and concrete PGlite adapter with workspace-scoped
  style/version reads, active-catalog and ordered-version listing, and explicit optimistic
  `ABANDONED` transition for an open version.
- Make `beginAnalysis` atomically change an eligible `DRAFT` or `FAILED` version to `ANALYZING`
  together with its exact `IMAGE_STYLE_VERSION` task/attempt/cost/outbox reservation. Replay must
  return the same result; typed failure must roll everything back.
- Enforce a deterministic version transition policy at the adapter boundary: direct draft saves
  cannot fabricate `ANALYZING`; analysis completion may move `ANALYZING` only to `NEEDS_REVIEW` or
  `FAILED`; review edits remain `NEEDS_REVIEW`; publish requires `NEEDS_REVIEW`; abandon requires a
  non-running open version; published/abandoned versions remain immutable.
- Before any profile enters `NEEDS_REVIEW` or `PUBLISHED`, require the exact
  `image-style-profile/v1` contract, validate its payload, and verify
  `SHA-256(RFC 8785 JCS(payload))` against the stored hash. Reject malformed, drifted, or wrong
  contract documents before mutation.
- Preserve v1 as active/selectable while v2 is open; only atomic v2 publication may move the parent
  pointer. Archiving hides a parent only for new-revision selection and preserves historical exact
  resolution.
- Add focused PGlite tests for workspace isolation, ordering, optimistic conflicts, transition
  rejection, atomic rollback/replay, profile/hash validation, abandon freeing the one-open-version
  constraint, publication immutability, and v1/v2 pointer behavior.
