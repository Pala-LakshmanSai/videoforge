## Causes and deployed changes

The supplied three-hand image already had the earlier mandatory hand-count/wrist rule. The scheduler's cyclic HANDS_ACTION role had pressured abstract homeowner/developer narration into an invented blueprint-pointing scene. Forbidden-content sanitization then erased the action and accepted generic filler. The trunk scene separately lacked physical person/camera placement. Successful image-file validation did not inspect visible anatomy.

- Required subject/action/environment now reject empty, forbidden or known placeholder facts instead of silently erasing meaning. Existing bounded replacement and cost/no-replay rules remain.
- Fresh V6/V7 schedules filter unsupported HANDS_ACTION candidates; they never turn another role into HANDS. Historical V2–V5 stay exact. New timing, layouts, IDs and random draws retain the V2/V5 parent namespace.
- New hosted prompt plans select physical-placement v2 / writer request v27. Whole-person handling instructions require visible torso/connected arms and person/object/camera position in authoritative scene fields. Genuine hand closeups and collaborators remain allowed. Legacy v24/v25 and experimental v26 builders remain frozen; saved plans recover the exact sealed policy before any dispatch.

No image provider/model, published style, video grammar, schema, GPU image or media-worker release changed. Manual output review remains required. **Automatic visual acceptance/repair is not implemented or activated.**
